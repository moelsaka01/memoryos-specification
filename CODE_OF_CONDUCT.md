# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The CCA project is committed to a professional, respectful, and inclusive
environment for contributors, reviewers, maintainers, and users. Participation
is evaluated on technical and collaborative conduct, not identity, background,
status, or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable, and evidence-based feedback.
- Ask questions when requirements are unclear and document ambiguity rather
  than assigning blame.
- Respect project scope, architecture decisions, and review boundaries.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and preserve an accurate engineering
  record.
- Protect private, confidential, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or intimidation.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Personal insults, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith.
- Knowingly misrepresenting test results, security posture, authorship, or
  project capabilities.

## Reporting and enforcement

Concerns should be reported privately to the designated project conduct contact
once that channel is established. Do not include sensitive details in a public
issue.

The identity of the conduct contact, the private reporting channel, the
enforcement authority, and the appeal process are currently unresolved project
governance decisions. This is a blocker for accepting a public community and is
recorded in [docs/ambiguity-register.md](docs/ambiguity-register.md). Until
those decisions are made, project owners are responsible for restricting or
pausing participation when needed to protect people and evidence.

Possible proportionate responses include a private correction, warning,
temporary participation restriction, removal from project spaces, or permanent
ban. Enforcement should be consistent, documented, confidential to the extent
practical, and free of retaliation.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, reviews, issue trackers, project
events, and other spaces where a person is representing the CCA project.
